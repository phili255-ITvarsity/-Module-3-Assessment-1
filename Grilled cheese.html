Grilledcheese sandwich

prep time:10 mins

yield:4

ingredients





steps

  1. stir together butter and parmeson cheese in a small bownl
  2. spread 1 1/2 tsp. butter mixture on 1 side of each bread slice.place 4 bread slices,buttered sides down,on wax paper. Top with provolone and mozzarella cheeses,top with remaining bread slices,butterd sides up.
  3. cook sandwich,in batches.on a hot griddle or in a non-stick over medium heat,gently pressing with a spatula, 4 minutes on each side or until golden brown and cheese is melted
